## Overview

I archive finished and discontinued initiatives so that collaborators can trace what was delivered, why it mattered, and where to find follow-up material (papers, packages, dashboards). Everything below is frozen: no active development, only reproducible notebooks, Docker images, or publications to consult.

### Highlights

- Benchmarked GBS pipelines (Reads2Map) and released tooling + Docker images for linkage-map studies.
- Built sustainability indexes (GHG, methane efficiency) that link emissions targets with breeding goals.
- Led analytics for sports and public-health applications, from NBA performance metrics to COVID-19 forecasting.
- Archived the multilevel sports-analytics framework (Oliveira–Newell score) after deploying it across seasons and teams.

## Timeline (most recent first)

### 2025 — Development and application of statistical frameworks in sports analytics *(archived)*
- **Focus:** Mixed-effects + PCA workflow (Oliveira–Newell score) for NBA seasons 2015–2019.
- **Deliverables:** Season/game dashboards, validation notebooks, reproducible scripts now frozen for reference.
- **Notes:** Superseded by newer sports-analytics engagements; code referenced in the Pipelines section.

### 2024 — Quantifying the drivers of genetic change in plant breeding
- **Focus:** Partition changes in genetic mean and variance to isolate breeding decisions that accelerate or hinder genetic gain.
- **Outputs:** Scenario analyses, management recommendations, and supporting scripts.

### 2023
- **Developing high-performance black soldier fly breeds for the insects-as-feed sector:** breeding dashboards + decision tools.
- **Hierarchical approach for evaluating athlete performance:** ON score paper, reproducible validation of athlete contributions.
- **Markerless vs marker-based systems:** agreement framework using functional limits and mixed models.

### 2022
- **AlphaPart – Partition of Breeding Values by Paths of Information:** package and workflows to quantify breeding contributions.
- **Genomic strategies for optimal crossbreeding in African dairy cattle:** simulation and decision-support artefacts.

### 2021
- **Use of biostatistics for optimising athlete performance:** athlete-specific readiness and health analytics.
- **Predictive models to forecast historical data-driven outcomes:** COVID-19 global forecasting, menstrual-cycle modelling.
- **Optimising selection and management of diversity in plant breeding:** temporal/genomic analysis of additive genetic variance.

### 2020
- **The lcc Package:** longitudinal concordance correlation software + publications.
- **Transmission efficiency of *Xylella fastidiosa*:** vector-behaviour studies and manuscripts.

### 2019
- **Sugarcane straw management for bioenergy:** climate and soil-carbon analyses.
- **Measuring colour using image analysis:** image-analysis methodology for horticulture.
